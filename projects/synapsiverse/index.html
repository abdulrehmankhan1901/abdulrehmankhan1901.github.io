Synapsiverse

A persistent multiplayer 3D space for social interaction and virtual events — my first professional Unity role.

UnityC#Fish Networking

Synapsiverse is a metaverse-style platform at Synapsify built for social interaction, digital events, and brand engagement. As Associate Game Developer, I was responsible for multiplayer implementation — building the systems that let users coexist and communicate inside a persistent 3D space, using Fish Networking in Unity.

Responsibilities

  • Multiplayer logic
  • Player-to-player text chat
  • Global text chat
  • Voice chat
  • Testing & optimization

Outcome

Delivered the full chat stack in a live production codebase and gained real experience working inside a fast-paced, feature-driven pipeline. Came away with a much stronger grasp of how persistent networked environments are structured — and what it takes to ship in a team setting.
