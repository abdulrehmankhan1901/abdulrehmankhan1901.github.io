Lightseeker

A third-person 3D action RPG built solo in Unreal Engine 5 — from combat systems to boss AI — as my final-year capstone.

Unreal Engine 5C++Blueprints

Lightseeker is a third-person action RPG built for Windows in Unreal Engine 5 as my final-year capstone. My teammate handled documentation and research so I could dedicate the full development time to building the game — every system, from player movement to the boss fight, was designed and implemented by me.

Responsibilities

  • Gameplay programming
  • Player movement & combat
  • Enemy movement & combat
  • Animation mapping
  • UI design
  • Sound integration
  • Game mechanics (health, stamina, pickups)

Outcome

Shipped a complete ARPG experience with a functional boss encounter, full player combat loop, and a polished game world. The project sharpened my skills in systems design, animation pipelines, and AI behavior — and proved I could take a game from blank project to finished product on my own.
